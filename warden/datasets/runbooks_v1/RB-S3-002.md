# RB-S3-002: High-volume object retrieval

## Purpose

Applies when a principal reads an unusual quantity of objects from a data store in a short period. Bulk reads are how data leaves. They are also how analysts, reporting jobs and backups work, so the volume threshold fires far more often on routine activity than on theft.

## Investigation steps

Ask whether this volume is normal for this principal against this bucket. A data analyst pulling from an analytics lake is routine; the same person pulling from a customer store is not. Look at pacing and sequence: a listing followed by systematic retrieval of everything is a sweep, while a handful of specific keys is a task. Compare the source network with the baseline, and check for a preceding permission change that made the read possible.

## Benign explanations

Scheduled reporting, model training runs, analyst investigations within their team's data, and migrations. The bucket is one the principal's team owns, the timing fits working hours, and the volume sits inside past ranges.

## Containment

If the reads look like exfiltration, suspend the principal's sessions and restrict the bucket to its owning role. Both need approval. Notify the data owner, as the reads may trigger disclosure obligations.

## Evidence to capture

Representative read events with object keys, total volume and duration, the principal's history against the same bucket, and any permission change beforehand.
